A release that adds the first JAudio and the Wii's and the Wii U's JAudio games. dolphinsf2 converts the music of The Wind Waker, on the GameCube and in The Wind Waker HD. It converts Twilight Princess on the GameCube and the Wii, Twilight Princess HD, Link's Crossbow Training, and the menu of The Legend of Zelda: Collector's Edition. Four Swords Adventures and the MusyX games convert as in 1.1.0.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop a game's disc image, an extracted disc's `main.dol`, or a `.baa` or `.aaf` file onto `dolphinsf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `dolphinsf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Dolphinsf2.app`. Drop inputs onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## New

- **The first JAudio.** The Wind Waker plays its sound through the first generation of JAudio. Its start-up archive is an `.aaf` file. Its sequences use the first command set, and its wave archives are in a `Banks` folder. dolphinsf2 reads the files and plays the sequences with the same player as JAudio2's. The Wind Waker HD keeps its sequences in a sequence table and its sounds' names in a name file. dolphinsf2 reads those too. The game's sound effects play in one sequence that the game's code steers. dolphinsf2 leaves that sequence out.
- **Wii and Wii U games.** dolphinsf2 converts the files of Wii and Wii U games that use JAudio. It doesn't read their discs or titles. Give it the game's start-up archive from the game's files. The README says where to find it.
- **Names.** A sequence of a game with the first JAudio takes its name from the game's name file, or else from its file in the sequence archive. Where a sound without a name plays the same file as an earlier sound at the same volume, the sequence is listed once.

## Known limitations

- The Wii and the Wii U play JAudio's music at 32,000 Hz, not at the GameCube's 32,028 Hz. There the music plays 0.089% slower and 1.5 cents lower than in the files.
- The first JAudio's sound effects don't convert.
- In the first JAudio, a track that shares its parent's voices gets separate voices. Oscillators that a track sets are left out. So are the tempos of child tracks. dolphinsf2 reports all three.
- The rest are as in 1.1.0. See "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
