First release of dolphinsf2, a MIDI, SoundFont 2 and SFZ converter for Nintendo GameCube games that use JAudio2. It executes each sequence using the game's playback rules and records the result. Each sequence gets a MIDI file and a SoundFont containing its instruments. The SFZ files hold the same instruments at the game's level.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later, with no additional runtime DLLs. Drop a game's disc image or its `.baa` file onto `dolphinsf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `dolphinsf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS and Linux, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Dolphinsf2.app`. Drop inputs onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## What it does

- **Input.** Reads GameCube disc images (`.iso`, `.gcm` and NKit images) or a game's start-up archive (`.baa`) with its `Seqs` and `Waves` folders. Compressed images (RVZ, WIA, GCZ and CISO) have to be converted to ISO with Dolphin first.
- **Sequence playback.** Follows JAudio2's sequence player: all 96 commands, registers, ports, interrupts and tracks that open other tracks. Uses the game's timing of 80-sample sub-frames, its 64 voices with their priorities and its random generator. The loop markers go on a pass of the main loop that a player looping on them repeats as the game plays the next pass.
- **MIDI.** Each sequence track gets a MIDI track and channel of its own. Volume, pan, pitch bend, vibrato depth and the effect send become controllers.
- **SoundFont.** Each combination of instrument and track settings becomes a preset, with the game's samples, tuning, volumes, envelopes and loops. Banks of both JAudio2 versions are read. Output targets FluidSynth.
- **SFZ.** One file per MIDI track, with WAV samples, at the game's level. Output targets sfizz 1.2.
- **Reports.** Lists approximations required by MIDI, SoundFont or SFZ for each sequence.
- **Known to work** with The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures.

## Known limitations

- Games with the first JAudio, such as The Wind Waker, aren't supported.
- MIDI pitch bend applies to a whole channel. Sweeps and tied notes bend their note's channel. Pitch envelopes are left out.
- SoundFont and SFZ envelopes are fitted to the game's oscillators. They can't follow an oscillator that rises again or loops.
- Random instrument effects play at their base value. Tremolo, track filters and the DSP's built-in oscillators are left out.
- Synth interpolation differs from the DSP's resampling.
- See "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
