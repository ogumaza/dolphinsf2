dolphinsf2 1.9.0 converts the stage music of Donkey Kong Jungle Beat with the chord effects that the enemies play beside it.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `dolphinsf2-1.9.0-win-x64.zip` | Windows x64 executable, `README.md`, `LICENSE` and `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` |

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop a game's disc image, an extracted disc's `main.dol`, or a `.baa` or `.aaf` file onto `dolphinsf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `dolphinsf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Dolphinsf2.app`. Drop inputs onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## New in 1.9.0

- **Donkey Kong Jungle Beat's chord layers.** Enemies and other objects of the stages loop 16 of the game's chord effects beside the stage music. The game works out the keys of their notes from the music's chords as it plays. dolphinsf2 now follows that code. It converts each stage's music once more with each such effect, and the music of two boss fights too: 135 files on the GameCube and 135 on the Wii. A file takes the name of the piece and the effect, such as `JA_BGM_MAIN_01_enemy_sleeping`. The effect starts with the piece, plays its steps on the music's beats and takes its keys from the music's chords. These files require the game's whole `AudioRes/Info` folder beside the start-up archive's folders. Without it, dolphinsf2 leaves them out and says so.
- **Super Mario Sunshine's music with Yoshi.** Nine pieces convert once more with the track that the game turns on while Mario rides Yoshi, such as `MSD_BGM_RICCO_YOSHI`.
- **Pikmin's music with enemies and in the evening.** tutorial, play3, yaku, cave and flow convert once more with the tracks that the game plays when an enemy comes near, in the evening, and in both: 15 files, such as `cave_evening`.
- **Pikmin 2's boss music, cave music and echo.** Each part of m_boss and l_boss converts as a separate file, such as `m_boss-3`. l_boss plays with the final boss in place: tracks 14 and 15 are off in place of 10 and 11, and parts 4 and 11 convert once more with track 15 on. The cave music's tracks send to the game's echo in CC91, and a sequence that sends to the echo ends when the echo falls silent.
- **The libraries.** dolphinsf2 now builds on ntdlib 7.0.0, with xsflib 1.12.0 and seqlib 1.5.0.

## Fixed in 1.9.0

- **Donkey Kong Jungle Beat's effects at the game's levels.** The game's audio manager sets a volume for each category of effects, from 1.1 to 1.35. dolphinsf2 now applies it. The effects play louder than in 1.8.0, and the new chord layers play at the same levels. The music stays the same.

## Documentation

- The notes of 1.8.0 said that dolphinsf2 converts the sound effects in the bank file of New Play Control! Pikmin on the Wii. It doesn't. dolphinsf2 doesn't read Wii disc images or extracted Wii discs, and that game has no start-up archive to give it in their place.
- The README now lists the three start-up archives of Twilight Princess's Wii releases, and the one that Twilight Princess HD shares with them.

## Known limitations

- The sound effects of Animal Crossing, its Japanese releases and Final Fantasy Crystal Chronicles aren't converted.
- Donkey Kong Jungle Beat works out the keys of 74 effects from the chords of its music as it plays. dolphinsf2 follows that code for the 16 that loop beside the stage music. On their own, 66 of those effects come out with no notes.
- The rest are as in 1.8.0. See "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
