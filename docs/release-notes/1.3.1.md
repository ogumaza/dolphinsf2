A release that corrects the SoundFonts' levels in FluidSynth and BASSMIDI. In those players, each zone now plays at its full attenuation. The SFZ files follow the opcodes' documentation at sfzformat.com more closely. The MIDI files and samples stay the same. Convert a game again to get the new SoundFonts and SFZ files.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop a game's disc image, an extracted disc's `main.dol`, or a `.baa` or `.aaf` file onto `dolphinsf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `dolphinsf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Dolphinsf2.app`. Drop inputs onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## Changes in 1.3.1

- **SoundFont attenuation.** FluidSynth, BASSMIDI and the E-mu chips apply 0.4 of a zone's initial attenuation. The specification calls for all of it. dolphinsf2 now writes 2.5 times each zone's attenuation in the generator, up to the generator's limit of 1440 cB. Those players play that limit as 57.6 dB. A modulator without a source holds any attenuation beyond 57.6 dB. FluidSynth 2.4.5 and later apply that modulator. BASSMIDI and earlier versions of FluidSynth ignore it. The JAudio games' attenuated zones used to play too loudly in those players. The MusyX games' zones used to hold all their attenuation in the modulator. BASSMIDI and FluidSynth 2.4.4 and earlier played them without it. In FluidSynth 2.4.5 and later, the MusyX games sound as before. A player that follows the specification now plays every attenuated zone too quietly.
- **SoundFont pans.** Each instrument's global zone now contains a modulator from CC10 to pan with an amount of 500. It takes the place of the default modulator. The specification gives the default an amount of 1000. Every player surveyed applies it at 500. Pans therefore sound as before in those players. A player that follows the specification now pans as they do.
- **MusyX voices at a volume of 0.** Such a voice used to play at full level in the SoundFont and in sfizz. Its zone now takes the largest attenuation that a SoundFont can give. Its SFZ region takes a volume of -144 dB, the lowest that the documentation gives. Both now play silent. In the game, such a voice plays only at a small volume addition. dolphinsf2 leaves volume additions out.
- **SFZ decays and releases.** The SFZ files now give decay and release times for the documentation's curve. That curve falls by a factor of e^-8 over the specified time: 69.49 dB. The times used to suit sfizz's fall of e^-9: 78.17 dB. Each time is now 8/9 of what it was. The MusyX games' pitch envelopes change the same way. In a player that follows the documentation, decays and releases fall at the game's rate. sfizz 1.2.3 ends them sooner. Where the game has fallen 40 dB, sfizz has fallen 45 dB.
- **SFZ opcodes.** Bend ranges are in whole cents. The JAudio games' velocity ranges start at 1. A velocity of 0 is a note-off. Both follow their documentation at sfzformat.com. Neither change can be heard.
- **The README on SFZ.** The SFZ section names sfizz 1.2 as the player tested. A new list gives the ways in which the opcodes depart from their documentation: long decays and loud regions. The README also describes sfizz's faster fall of decays and releases.
- **The README on SoundFont players.** The README says that the SoundFont output departs from the specification in the zones' attenuation. It describes the new form of that attenuation and how TinySoundFont plays it.
- **The libraries.** dolphinsf2 now builds on seqlib 1.3.0, xsflib 1.5.0 and ntdlib 1.11.0.

## Known limitations

As in 1.3.0, apart from the changes above. A player that follows the SoundFont specification plays attenuated zones too quietly. sfizz 1.2.3 ends decays and releases sooner than the game. See "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
