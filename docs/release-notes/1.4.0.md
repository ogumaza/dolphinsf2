dolphinsf2 1.4.0 converts the sound effects of The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker, on the GameCube and in The Wind Waker HD, and of Pac-Man Vs. The release also has fixes to the music of several MusyX and JAudio games.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop a game's disc image, an extracted disc's `main.dol`, or a `.baa` or `.aaf` file onto `dolphinsf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `dolphinsf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Dolphinsf2.app`. Drop inputs onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## New in 1.4.0

- **The sound effects of The Wind Waker and Pac-Man Vs.** These games play all their sound effects in one sequence: the first sequence of the sound table. The sound library starts an effect by writing ports of one of that sequence's slots. dolphinsf2 used to leave the sequence out. dolphinsf2 now converts each effect as that sequence plays it when the game starts the effect. dolphinsf2 recognizes these games by the start-up archives of their USA releases. The effects come after the music, in the sound table's order. An effect that the game doesn't name becomes `se<category>-<index>`, such as `se4-0123`. An effect plays at its volume in the sound table times the volume of its category. Each effect also plays at the pitch that the table gives it. The MIDI file holds that pitch as a pitch bend. The README's new section "The first JAudio's sound effects" describes the effects.
- **`--port` for sound effects.** For a sound effect of the first JAudio, `--port` writes a port of the effect's slot. The game's code does the same when it passes values with an effect. A value for port 6 stands for the map's information, such as the ground under a footstep. Some effects play another sound for each value there. Without a value, some of them play nothing.
- **The DSP's automatic mixer.** A track of the first JAudio can send its notes through the DSP's automatic mixer. That mixer sets their gains from their volume and pan. dolphinsf2 now plays those notes at half the level of the others. That level approximates the game's mix. The Wind Waker's sound effects and some of its music use the mixer. That music used to play too loudly against the rest.
- **Four Swords Adventures' JA_BGM_SELECT.** When the piece starts, the game's code mutes all of its tracks but two. dolphinsf2 now leaves out the notes of the muted tracks. The piece used to play every track.
- **MusyX's volume additions.** MusyX's ScaleVolume step scales a voice's volume, then adds to it. dolphinsf2 used to leave the addition out. A voice's zone now takes the level of the scaled volume plus the addition. Such a voice follows the velocity less than the default curves do. Its zone holds a velocity modulator in place of the SoundFont's default one. Its SFZ region gives its velocity curve. The NDDEMO disc's songs play such voices. Some of them used to play too quietly or not at all.
- **Fades after a release.** Many of the NDDEMO disc's macros fade a voice out after its note's release, then stop it. dolphinsf2 used to cut such a voice 10 ms after the release. The voice's zone now gets a release that reaches 20 dB down when the macro's fade does. The song's end takes in the fade.
- **Fades of held notes.** A macro can also fade a voice while its note is held. That fade now shapes the zone's volume envelope. A held note that the game fades out used to play on at its level until its release. A voice that stops at the same time whether or not its note is released now ignores the release.
- **Notes of no length.** A MusyX note of length 0 ends on the tick it starts. Its note-off used to come before its note-on in the MIDI file. The note then never ended. The note-off now comes after the note-on.
- **Messages and the app.** For a folder among the inputs, dolphinsf2's message now ends "give its .baa or .aaf file, or an extracted disc's sys/main.dol". The macOS app's description and its file dialog name the main.dol of an extracted disc too.
- **The README.** A new section, "Game rules", describes Four Swords Adventures' rules: the system sounds' volume, the fade of a note without a release, the end of a sequence that sends to the echo and the muted tracks. The section also gives the volumes of The Wind Waker's categories of sound effects.
- **The libraries.** dolphinsf2 now builds on ntdlib 1.12.0 and xsflib 1.6.0, with seqlib 1.3.0.

## Known limitations

- MusyX games that the README doesn't list don't convert. Neither do the sound effects of the MusyX games.
- dolphinsf2 converts the sound effects of the USA releases of The Wind Waker, The Wind Waker HD and Pac-Man Vs. Other releases of those games convert without their effects. So do the other games that use the first JAudio. Pikmin 2 is one of them: it starts its effects another way.
- dolphinsf2 plays each of The Wind Waker's sound effects in the first slot of its category. The game's code passes values with some effects, such as the material of the ground under a footstep. A conversion passes none unless `--port` gives them. Some effects then play nothing. A game keeps some effects going by starting them again and again. dolphinsf2 plays each effect from one start: to its end, or with loop markers where it loops.
- Notes on the DSP's automatic mixer play at half the level of the others. That level approximates the game's mix.
- The rest are as in 1.3.1, apart from the changes above. See "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
