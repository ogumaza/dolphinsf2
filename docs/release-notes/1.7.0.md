dolphinsf2 1.7.0 converts the music of Animal Crossing and its two Japanese releases. They play it through a port of Nintendo's sound library for the Nintendo 64. dolphinsf2 also converts the music of Final Fantasy Crystal Chronicles, from Square's Red driver, and ten more MusyX games, among them Star Fox Adventures, Rogue Leader, Resident Evil and the two Metroid Prime games. Luigi's Mansion, Mario Kart: Double Dash!!, Donkey Kong Jungle Beat, Super Mario Sunshine and Pikmin convert too, and Pikmin 2's music now plays. The Wind Waker's and Twilight Princess's music converts in the states that the games give it. dolphinsf2 converts the music and sound effects of Nintendo GameCube games to MIDI, SoundFont 2 and SFZ files.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop a game's disc image, an extracted disc's `main.dol`, or a `.baa` or `.aaf` file onto `dolphinsf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `dolphinsf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Dolphinsf2.app`. Drop inputs onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## New in 1.7.0

- **The Nintendo 64's sound library.** Animal Crossing (USA), Doubutsu no Mori+ and Doubutsu no Mori e+ (disc versions 0 and 1 of each) play their music through a port of the library. dolphinsf2 converts it. Each piece is named `bgm` and its number. A piece of the hour gets a file for each kind of weather, the museum's piece one for each room, and the island's piece one with and one without the islander. The music discs play at the stereo's volume with every pan in the centre. Kapp'n's songs play in two parts. The library's envelopes fade in lines in amplitude. A zone's hold and decay follow each fade down to 20 dB, and its release follows the first 14 dB. Where a fade falls more slowly than SoundFont's slowest decay, the zone in the SoundFont holds for up to about 18 s and then falls at the slowest rate. The hold brings the zone closest to the game's fade. A legato phrase plays as one MIDI note, with pitch bends for the phrase and its sweeps. `--seed` sets the library's random numbers. The games' sound effects aren't converted.
- **Square's Red driver.** Final Fantasy Crystal Chronicles (USA) plays its music through it. dolphinsf2 converts each piece in `dvd/sound/Music` with its bank in `dvd/sound/Wave` and names it after its file. It follows the driver's tempo, notes, loop, levels, pans, splits and envelopes. A piece with more tracks than MIDI has channels shares channels between the tracks that conflict least. The driver's sound effects aren't converted.
- **Ten more MusyX games.** Star Fox Adventures, Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door, Pokémon Channel, Star Wars Rogue Squadron II: Rogue Leader, Star Wars Rogue Squadron III: Rebel Strike, Resident Evil, Resident Evil Zero, Tales of Symphonia, Metroid Prime and Metroid Prime 2: Echoes keep their sounds in layouts of their own. dolphinsf2 reads each game's list of songs and effects from its code or its files. A song plays with the groups that the game pushes when it starts the song. Rogue Leader's music hall names its songs. Resident Evil Zero's cutscenes play some of their effects as songs, and each of those converts with the cutscene's name and the song's ID. Resident Evil's six mixes that rooms give two ambiences convert as files of their own, such as `sg74 ch1`. The README lists what each game converts.
- **Five more JAudio games.** dolphinsf2 converts the music and sound effects of Luigi's Mansion, Mario Kart: Double Dash!!, Donkey Kong Jungle Beat, Super Mario Sunshine and Pikmin, from their USA releases. Double Dash!! keeps its music in the start-up archive and its voices in three more start-up archives. Pikmin has an earlier form of the first JAudio, with its sequence index in the game's code. Luigi's Mansion's LuigiSings converts in 13 states: the hallway, a room, outdoors and a room without accompaniment, each at 100, 70 and 40 HP, and a lit room. dolphinsf2 follows each game's rules for its music, such as Jungle Beat's muted tracks, Yoshi's track in Super Mario Sunshine and Pikmin's track volumes and cutscene music.
- **Pikmin 2's music.** The game plays each piece through an object of its sound layer. dolphinsf2 now gives the pieces the layer's answers, mutes the tracks that follow the game's action, and plays each cave piece with its conductor. Most of the music came out with no notes in 1.6.0.
- **The Wind Waker's music in the game's states.** The game mutes some tracks of 14 pieces as it starts them. dolphinsf2 starts each piece with the tracks that the game plays. Each other state converts as a file of its own, such as `JA_BGM_KAMI_TOWER_80000125` or `JA_BGM_MBOSS_PORT9_2`. The battle music and three other pieces fade in over two seconds, as they do in the game. Four effects that pick a random key convert once for each key that makes a sound. The European and Japanese releases convert as the American one does, effects included, on the GameCube and in The Wind Waker HD.
- **Twilight Princess's music in the game's states.** The game mutes some tracks of five pieces as it starts them. Hyrule Field's day music holds a walking and a riding arrangement, and each converts. The game's scenes and fights change the tracks of 17 pieces, and each later state converts as a file of its own, such as `Z2BGM_TOAL_VILLEGE_status1`. The European and Japanese releases convert as the American one does. The categories of effects play at the game's volumes. The music of Twilight Princess and Link's Crossbow Training ends when the game's echo falls silent.
- **Four Swords Adventures' second set of tracks.** The game plays `JA_BGM_SELECT` with one set of its tracks on the file select and another on the map. `JA_BGM_SELECT_map` holds the second set. The European release converts too.
- **Mario Party 4's other releases.** The European and Japanese releases convert, and so does disc version 0 of the American one. The Japanese release names its songs and voices in Japanese, as its sound test does.
- **More of MusyX's macros.** dolphinsf2 now follows MusyX's Loop, FadeIn, Gosub and Return steps, its variables, VolumeSelect, ReverbSelect and PitchWheelSelect, and the splits by the mod wheel and the velocity. Envelopes that a macro takes from the channel's controllers play, such as Pokémon Colosseum's and Star Fox Adventures'. A channel whose notes start at several sets of values, or on both sides of a split, plays a preset for each. A song's note whose voice sweeps its pitch bends on a separate MIDI channel. An effect's note follows its voice's pitch sweeps, key changes and gliding pans. A voice that asks for more than the DSP's highest pitch plays at it. A voice that plays on after its note's release sounds until its KeyOff or until it stops.
- **MusyX's songs.** Star Fox Adventures and Resident Evil Zero's cutscenes fade their songs in, and the files do too. A song's linear releases are cut once the game's voices of the channel have all fallen silent. A note before a program change on its tick plays the earlier program. A song whose sections loop at different lengths gets loop markers only where every section comes back to the same place.
- **Track filters.** A JAudio music track's IIR filter becomes two zones for each region. One plays the region unfiltered, and the other plays it through a low-pass. Their levels together follow the filter's curve.
- **JAudio's track envelopes and random numbers.** A track's simple envelope plays in The Wind Waker, Pac-Man Vs. and Pikmin 2. The first JAudio draws its random numbers from another generator than JAudio2's. `--seed` sets JAudio2's generator. Luigi's Mansion's sounds draw from it too.
- **More inputs.** dolphinsf2 decodes 2-bit ADPCM waves, such as Super Mario Sunshine's voices of its townsfolk. It reads start-up archives that Yaz0 compresses (`.szs`), and the wave archives from `Banks` where `Waves` is missing, and the other way round.
- **The libraries.** dolphinsf2 now builds on ntdlib 3.2.0, with xsflib 1.8.0 and seqlib 1.3.0.

## Fixed in 1.7.0

- **Chords across a split.** Where notes of one tick took different presets of a split or of a channel's controllers, every note of the tick played the last note's preset. Each note now plays the preset of its side. Rogue Leader's songs change.

## Known limitations

- The sound effects of Animal Crossing, its Japanese releases and Final Fantasy Crystal Chronicles aren't converted.
- Donkey Kong Jungle Beat works out the keys of 74 effects from the chords of its music as it plays. dolphinsf2 doesn't follow that code. 66 of those effects come out with no notes.
- Pikmin 2's cave music picks its phrases at random. Its file plays until each track reaches its last part and has no loop. Many of Pikmin 2's effects play quieter than in the game.
- Rogue Leader plays 68 of its songs from streams. dolphinsf2 leaves out the 60 songs that play only a stream and the 8 that play one along with their notes. Paper Mario, Rebel Strike and the two Metroid Prime games stream most of their music.
- Many of the two Metroid Prime games' effects pick a random key or a random branch of their macro. dolphinsf2 plays the note's key and the first branch.
- Resident Evil's rooms set the levels of some songs' channels while they play. Each file plays at the levels of its song, apart from the six mixes. The game gives each song a reverb preset. Synths play the songs' CC91 through their reverb.
- The Wind Waker's and Twilight Princess's states each convert from the piece's start. The game moves the tracks to a new state gradually.
- MusyX reads a channel's controllers when a macro's step runs. The files take them at the note's start.
- A Red piece with more tracks than MIDI has channels shares channels between tracks. Where tracks with different settings share one, dolphinsf2 reports it.
- The rest are as in 1.6.0, apart from the changes above. See "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
