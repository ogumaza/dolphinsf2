dolphinsf2 1.8.0 converts Donkey Kong Jungle Beat's music once more in each state that the game sets during play.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `dolphinsf2-1.8.0-win-x64.zip` | Windows x64 executable, `README.md`, `LICENSE` and `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` |

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop a game's disc image, an extracted disc's `main.dol`, or a `.baa` or `.aaf` file onto `dolphinsf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `dolphinsf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Dolphinsf2.app`. Drop inputs onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## New in 1.8.0

- **Donkey Kong Jungle Beat's music in the game's states.** The game turns tracks on or off during play: as a combo grows, while a Helibird carries DK, in each part of a boss fight, while DK swims deep in TropicalCoast, and in a few scenes. dolphinsf2 converts 43 pieces once more in each such state, 101 files in all. New Play Control! Donkey Kong Jungle Beat on the Wii adds one more, for its menus. Each file plays its state from the start of the piece. The game fades from one state to the next, and the files leave out those fades. The name of each file is the name of the piece with a suffix for the state:
  - `_combo1` and `_combo2`: the layers of a combo, as in `JA_BGM_MAIN_01_combo1`
  - `_helibird`, `_helibird_combo1` and `_helibird_combo2`: a flight with a Helibird, alone or in a combo
  - `_below_75`, `_change`, `_round2` and `_round2_below_30`: the fights against the Kongs, with the boss below 75% of its health, between the rounds, in the second round, and below 30% there
  - `_hit`, `_change`, `_round2` and `_round2_hit`: the fights against GoroPig, ElephantCannon and SlumpBird, while DK hits or holds the boss, between the rounds, in the second round, and while DK hits or holds the boss there
  - `_below_60`, `_below_30`, `_hold`, `_below_60_hold` and `_below_30_hold`: the final fight, by the boss's health, and while DK holds on to the boss
  - `_fall`: the lead-in to the final fight, while DK falls from the Helibird
  - `_underwater`: TropicalCoast's music while DK swims below
  - `_state1` and `_state2`: the other scenes, such as the ending ceremony
- **New Play Control! Pikmin's effects.** dolphinsf2 now converts the sound effects in the bank file of New Play Control! Pikmin on the Wii, as it does for the GameCube release.
- **The libraries.** dolphinsf2 now builds on ntdlib 3.6.0, with xsflib 1.10.0 and seqlib 1.4.0.
- **Builds on Windows.** The build now stops with a message for a Windows compiler other than clang-cl, such as cl.exe from MSVC or GCC from MinGW. The README gives the steps for clang-cl.

## Fixed in 1.8.0

- **Read errors with the right file.** When a conversion failed after a bank or a wave couldn't be read, the read error appeared with the next file. That file might not use the bank. The message of a failed conversion now ends with the read errors.
- **Names in any language on Windows.** dolphinsf2 couldn't convert an input whose path held characters outside the Windows code page, such as a Japanese folder name. It now reads and writes such paths on Windows 10 version 1903 and later.
- **Extracted discs with a folder that can't be opened.** dolphinsf2 stopped listing an extracted disc's `files` folder at the first subfolder that it wasn't allowed to open. It gave no message and left out the files after that folder. It now goes on past such a folder.
- **A finished track's port.** A JAudio2 sequence that read a port of a child track after the track had finished took the track's last value. The game reads nothing then, and dolphinsf2 now does the same.
- **Settings after a reset of all controllers.** A MusyX song that reset all controllers (CC121) and then set a controller or the pitch bend to its value before the reset lost that setting. A bent note then played unbent, and the controller stayed at its default in players that reset it. A reset right after another reset was left out too. Both now go in the MIDI file. Star Fox Adventures has such songs.
- **High one-shot notes.** A MusyX voice that asks for more than the DSP's highest pitch plays at that pitch, and its zones in the SoundFont already did the same. dolphinsf2 still timed the voice's one-shot sample at the pitch asked for. The note could then end before its sample did. The note now lasts as long as the sample plays.
- **Final Fantasy Crystal Chronicles' first channel.** In a piece with more than 16 tracks, the tracks without notes go on channel 0. dolphinsf2 counted them as sharing the channel with the track that plays notes there. That track then sent its volume, pan and other controllers only when its notes started. It now sends them when the piece sets them, as the game does.

## Changed messages

dolphinsf2 now shares more of its readers with ntdlib. Some messages for damaged input changed with them:

- A table that main.dol is missing is named in the message. In 1.7.0, each of MusyX's tables gave "main.dol has no data at the song list's address". It's now "main.dol has no data at the song list", "main.dol has no data at the sound room", "main.dol has no data at the table of effects" and so on.
- Animal Crossing's "main.dol has no table of … where the game's sound library reads it" and "main.dol's table of … ends early" are now "main.dol has no data at the table of …".
- An Animal Crossing script that writes past the end of its sequence now stops the conversion with "a script writes past the end of sequence N".
- Pokémon Colosseum's "… has no file for …'s sound N" and "…'s sound N names no song group" are now "bgm_archive.fsys and common.fsys have no files for sound N". "…'s sound N names no effects group" is now "common.fsys has no group for sound N".
- A song or effect whose group is missing from the game's project now gives "no group that the game pushes plays …" in place of "… has no song group N" or "….prj has no song group N".
- Nintendo Puzzle Collection's "… has no files for song N's group" is now "the menu has no files for the group of its song N", and "…'s song N has no group of songs" is now "Yoshi's Cookie's song N has no group". Parts or items that overlap no longer stop the conversion at once. The part reads as empty, and its reader reports the error.
- Damaged Yay0 data gives "a compressed block's bits leave the data", "a compressed block leaves the data" or "a compressed block repeats bytes before its start" in place of "the Yay0 data is damaged" and "the Yay0 data refers to bytes before its start". "the Yay0 data unpacks to more than 64 MiB" is now "Yay0 data decodes to more than 64 MiB".

## Known limitations

- The sound effects of Animal Crossing, its Japanese releases and Final Fantasy Crystal Chronicles aren't converted.
- Donkey Kong Jungle Beat works out the keys of 74 effects from the chords of its music as it plays. dolphinsf2 doesn't follow that code. 66 of those effects come out with no notes.
- Pikmin 2's cave music picks its phrases at random. Its file plays until each track reaches its last part and has no loop. Many of Pikmin 2's effects play quieter than in the game.
- Rogue Leader plays 68 of its songs from streams. dolphinsf2 leaves out the 60 songs that play only a stream and the 8 that play one along with their notes. Paper Mario, Rebel Strike and the two Metroid Prime games stream most of their music.
- Many of the two Metroid Prime games' effects pick a random key or a random branch of their macro. dolphinsf2 plays the note's key and the first branch.
- Resident Evil's rooms set the levels of some songs' channels while they play. Each file plays at the levels of its song, apart from the six mixes. The game gives each song a reverb preset. Synths play the songs' CC91 through their reverb.
- The states of The Wind Waker, Twilight Princess and Donkey Kong Jungle Beat each convert from the start of the piece. The game moves the tracks to a new state gradually.
- MusyX reads a channel's controllers when a macro's step runs. The files take them at the note's start.
- A Red piece with more tracks than MIDI has channels shares channels between tracks. Where tracks with different settings share one, dolphinsf2 reports it.
- The rest are as in 1.7.0, apart from the changes above. See "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
