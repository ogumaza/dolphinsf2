dolphinsf2 1.3.0 converts the music of five more MusyX games and reads compressed disc images. MusyX's voices now follow the volume table and the master volume that each game sets. The JAudio games convert as in 1.2.1.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop a game's disc image, an extracted disc's `main.dol`, or a `.baa` or `.aaf` file onto `dolphinsf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `dolphinsf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Dolphinsf2.app`. Drop inputs onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## New in 1.3.0

- **Five more MusyX games.** dolphinsf2 now converts the music of these games. It recognizes each one by its `main.dol`.
  - Pokémon Colosseum (USA). The game keeps its songs in FSYS archives, and LZSS compresses some of their files. Each song takes its file's name.
  - Mario Party 4 (USA, disc version 1): the songs of the sound test, with the sound test's names. The sound test's streams are recorded audio, not songs. They're left out.
  - WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$! (USA): the songs of the sound room, with its names.
  - Nintendo Puzzle Collection (Japan): the music of the menu, Dr. Mario, Panel de Pon and Yoshi's Cookie. The game keeps its music in the modules of its code.
  - The NDDEMO disc, a demo disc that never went on sale. Its early build of MusyX plays the songs 0.8% slower than their tempo says. The conversion plays them at that speed.
- **Compressed disc images.** dolphinsf2 reads RVZ, WIA, GCZ and CISO images. Before, it asked for them to be converted to ISO. It also reads a disc whose header leaves out the magic word, if the rest of the disc is intact.
- **MusyX's volume table.** MusyX plays a voice's volume through a table of levels in the game's code. dolphinsf2 reads the table and writes velocities and channel volumes whose squares follow it. The songs of Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour and Mario Power Tennis change slightly with it.
- **The master volume.** A game can set MusyX's master volume for music. dolphinsf2 now applies it inside the volume curve, as MusyX does. It replaces the fixed factor that 1.2.1 gave Mario Power Tennis's songs.
- **Pitch wheel ranges and pan by key.** A MusyX macro can set its voice's pitch wheel range and pan its voice by the key. dolphinsf2 follows both.
- **The attenuation of MusyX zones.** A MusyX zone's attenuation now goes in a modulator without a source. The SoundFont specification counts a missing source as 1. The modulator therefore adds its whole amount. FluidSynth applies only 0.4 of the attenuation generator, but from 2.4.5 on it applies all of such a modulator. FluidSynth 2.4.4 and earlier and BASSMIDI ignore the modulator. The zones then play without their attenuation. The JAudio games' zones keep the generator. The README's "Attenuation" explains it.
- **Compression libraries.** Compressed images need zlib, Zstandard, liblzma and bzip2. The build uses installed copies where it finds them, and downloads the others. The Windows program contains them. `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` lists them with their licences.
- **The libraries.** dolphinsf2 now builds on ntdlib 1.9.0, with seqlib 1.2.0 and xsflib 1.3.0.

## Known limitations

- MusyX games that the README doesn't list don't convert. Neither do the sound effects of the MusyX games.
- In MusyX, each voice bends over its pitch wheel range. A MIDI channel bends all its notes over one range. dolphinsf2 gives a channel the range of its latest note.
- MusyX looks up the product of a voice's volumes in its volume table. Players square the velocity, CC7 and CC11 one by one and multiply the results. The two can differ slightly. Volume changes during a note follow the players' squares.
- A bend or a vibrato can still take a note past the DSP's pitch limit. In the game, the note stays at the limit for as long as its pitch would pass it.
- The Wii and the Wii U play JAudio's music at 32,000 Hz, not at the GameCube's 32,028 Hz. There the music plays 0.089% slower and 1.5 cents lower than in the files.
- The first JAudio's sound effects don't convert.
- In the first JAudio, a track that shares its parent's voices gets separate voices. Oscillators that a track sets are left out. So are the tempos of child tracks. dolphinsf2 reports all three.
- The rest are as in 1.2.1. See "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
