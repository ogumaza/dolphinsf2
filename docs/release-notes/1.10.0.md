dolphinsf2 1.10.0 converts the music of Luigi's Mansion's boss fights in each state that the game plays, and LuigiSings without Luigi's humming.

## Downloads

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `dolphinsf2-1.10.0-win-x64.zip` | Windows x64 executable, `README.md`, `LICENSE` and `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` |

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop a game's disc image, an extracted disc's `main.dol`, or a `.baa` or `.aaf` file onto `dolphinsf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `dolphinsf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Dolphinsf2.app`. Drop inputs onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## New in 1.10.0

- **Luigi's Mansion's boss music in each state.** The music of four boss fights, BabyBattle (Chauncey), BlueBattle (Bogmire), TelesaBattle (Boolossus) and KuppaBattle (King Boo), changes its tracks as the fight goes on, and some of its states play faster. Each later state is now a file of its own, such as `BabyBattle_state_1`: states 1 and 2 of BabyBattle and BlueBattle, and state 1 of TelesaBattle and KuppaBattle.
- **LuigiSings without the humming.** While a message window is open, the game mutes Luigi's humming, and the accompaniment plays on alone. Three new files play that at 100 HP in the hallway, a room and outdoors, such as `LuigiSings_room_100_HP_no_humming`.
- **The libraries.** dolphinsf2 now builds on ntdlib 7.3.0, with xsflib 1.12.0 and seqlib 1.5.0.

## Fixed in 1.10.0

- **Luigi's Mansion's boss music.** The files of BabyBattle, BlueBattle, TelesaBattle and KuppaBattle played every track of the piece at once: the music of all its states together. Each piece's file now plays the tracks that the game plays as the fight starts.

## Other changes

- **File names of Luigi's Mansion.** Sounds whose names differ only in case get a number at the end of the name, their place in the list. The new files move some of those numbers. Eight files, such as those of Mario, Light and GetKey, have new numbers and the same contents.

## Documentation

- The README names the piano piece that Luigi hears in the hallway next to Melody Pianissima's room. It's the sound effect `se1-0169`, and every conversion contains it.

## Known limitations

- In TelesaBattle's state 1, the game raises the tempo as Luigi catches the small Boos of the fight, up to about 1.4 times. The file keeps the tempo that the state starts with.
- The rest are as in 1.9.0. See "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
