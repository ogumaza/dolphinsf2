A release with four fixes to the conversion of JAudio games. In some of Four Swords Adventures' music, notes past the DSP's pitch limit now play at the limit. The game plays them there too. Some sound effects of Twilight Princess, Link's Crossbow Training and Four Swords Adventures change as well. Mario Golf, Mario Power Tennis, The Wind Waker and Collector's Edition's menu convert as in 1.2.0.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop a game's disc image, an extracted disc's `main.dol`, or a `.baa` or `.aaf` file onto `dolphinsf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `dolphinsf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Dolphinsf2.app`. Drop inputs onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## Fixes

- **The DSP's pitch limit.** The DSP reads at most just under 8 samples of a wave for each sample of its output. The game holds a voice's pitch at that limit. dolphinsf2 played keys past the limit at their full pitch. They now play at the limit, from a separate zone with a scale tuning of 0. A bend or a vibrato still moves such a note.
- **A jump to the next command.** dolphinsf2 counted a jump to the command right after it as a jump back. The loop markers of a sequence with such a jump could then go in the wrong place. The game's player just moves on at such a jump. dolphinsf2 now does the same.
- **Envelope scaling.** JAudio's command 0xe7 scales a track's envelope. dolphinsf2 ignored it. The tracks that use it now play at the game's level. Their notes also end where the game ends them.
- **A wave archive that can't be read.** A read error while a wave archive loaded silenced the notes that followed. dolphinsf2 now reports the archive and converts the sequence without it.

## Other changes

- **FluidSynth's level.** The README explains a difference in FluidSynth's level. FluidSynth plays every note 1.5 dB louder than the SoundFont specifies. Multiplying FluidSynth's gain by 0.84 gives the specified level.
- **SoundFont padding.** A sample's unrolled loop is followed by 32 samples, not 8 as the README said.
- **The libraries.** dolphinsf2 now builds on seqlib 1.2.0, xsflib 1.3.0 and ntdlib 1.8.0.

## Known limitations

- A bend or a vibrato can still take a note past the DSP's pitch limit. In the game, the note stays at the limit for as long as its pitch would pass it.
- The Wii and the Wii U play JAudio's music at 32,000 Hz, not at the GameCube's 32,028 Hz. There the music plays 0.089% slower and 1.5 cents lower than in the files.
- The first JAudio's sound effects don't convert.
- In the first JAudio, a track that shares its parent's voices gets separate voices. Oscillators that a track sets are left out. So are the tempos of child tracks. dolphinsf2 reports all three.
- The rest are as in 1.2.0. See "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
