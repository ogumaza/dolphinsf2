A release that adds the music of two games that play it through Factor 5's MusyX: Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour and Mario Power Tennis, both from the USA. JAudio2 games convert as in 1.0.2.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later. No additional runtime DLLs are required. Drop a game's disc image, an extracted disc's `main.dol` or a `.baa` file onto `dolphinsf2.exe`. You can also use a terminal. `dolphinsf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Dolphinsf2.app`. Drop inputs onto the app in Finder to convert them.

## New

- **MusyX.** dolphinsf2 converts the music of Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour and Mario Power Tennis (USA). Both games play it through MusyX 2, Factor 5's sound system. dolphinsf2 reads the song table in each game's code. Each song plays as MusyX's sequencer plays it. The instruments come from the song group's SoundMacros, keymaps, layers and samples. As with JAudio2, each song gets a MIDI file, a SoundFont and SFZ files. The README's "MusyX" section has the details. The README's "Differences from the game" section lists what MIDI and SoundFont can't play.
- **Extracted discs.** dolphinsf2 reads a disc that Dolphin extracted. Give it the disc's `sys/main.dol`. A MusyX game converts from its disc image or from its extracted disc. A JAudio2 game converts from those too.
- **Options for JAudio2 only.** `--seed`, `--port` and `--voices` apply to JAudio2 games. MusyX conversion ignores them. The usage says so.

## Known limitations

- MusyX conversion covers the two games above. Other MusyX games don't convert. Neither do the sound effects of these two.
- Mario Power Tennis plays its songs more quietly than MusyX's volume model gives. The cause isn't known. dolphinsf2 scales the game's channel volumes by a fixed factor to match.
- In the game, a song's voices stop when the song ends. In the MIDI files, the last notes play their releases.
- The rest are as in 1.0.2. See "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
